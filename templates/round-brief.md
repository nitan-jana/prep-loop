# Round brief — <date>

A brief for one loop, at `instance/mocks/<date>-brief.md`. Written ahead of the
loop by the review session, per [`policy/mocks.md`](../policy/mocks.md#the-brief).

**This file is self-contained. It carries no links out** — not into the
profile, not into policy, not into the logs or the week plan. An interviewer
that is not this repo reads only this file, and every link out is something that
interviewer cannot see.

It is also the privacy boundary: the brief is the only thing that ever leaves
the machine, and only when the user sends it. Write in what a round needs and
nothing else — no scores, no resume, no readiness rungs.

## For the interviewer

One question, then silence. At least one follow-up on every answer, including a
good one. Never supply a term the candidate is groping for. Never mention a
grade, a rubric, or what a stronger answer would have contained — all of it
waits for the review.

Conduct and record. **Do not grade.**
