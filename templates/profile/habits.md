# Habits

Patterns in how this user answers, found by watching rounds.

**This file starts empty and stays empty until there are rounds to watch.**
Onboarding does not fill it in, and never asks the user to.

A weak interview habit cannot be self-reported. Asking someone what they do
badly under pressure returns a rehearsed answer — modest, plausible, and
usually about something else entirely. Worse, once it is written down, every
round afterwards is shaped by it: the interviewer pushes where the file says to
push, and the actual habit goes on being missed.

The habits worth having are the specific ones, and they only appear from the
outside: stopping once a mechanism is named without saying what it costs,
skipping the alternative that was rejected, going quiet instead of thinking out
loud, running long on the setup and short on the answer.
