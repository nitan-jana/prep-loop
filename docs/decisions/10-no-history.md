# Never say what was there before

Not "moved from an earlier day". Not "deferred from last week". Not "(which did
not happen)". Not a `Was` column in a deferred table. Not "third attempt at this
one". An item that slipped is carried forward as a plain instruction with no
account of how it got there.

## Why it is a hard rule rather than a style preference

A trail of displacement notes turns every artifact into a record of what the
user failed to do, and these get read every day. **A rule that would be pedantic
in a document read once is load-bearing in one read three hundred times.**

The information is not even useful. The planner can see the slip from the logs,
and the user gains nothing from being reminded of it at the top of the thing
they are about to work on.

It is one of three rules governing anything read daily. The other two — minimal,
and instructions never completion state — are in the same file and rule out
their own lists of concrete phrasings.

## The extension, and the test that came with it

The rule was later pushed past the daily artifacts to everything in the personal
folder, including the profile and the resource inventories. Those are not read
daily, so minimalism does not bind them and a reference file may explain itself
at length. But a file rewritten every few months accumulates an account of its
own earlier versions just as readily as a week plan does, and it is read at
exactly the moments something is being planned or drilled.

That needed a test, because some knowledge about the past is load-bearing and
the rule has to keep it. **Does the sentence instruct, or does it narrate?**

| Instructs, so it stays | Narrates, so it goes |
|---|---|
| The index pages omit a whole section; it appears only in the sitemap | The earlier pull read the index pages and missed that section |
| A slug on this source differs from the one it is known by elsewhere | The last version invented that slug and it was wrong |

Left column tells the next session what to do. Right column tells a reader what
happened, and the reader it is telling is the person who will act on it, who
gains nothing from the account and reads a small failure notice instead.

**Correcting a file is not an event worth recording. Write the file as though it
were the first version of itself.**

## What it does not touch

The log. That is the one place the past belongs, and even there it is recorded
as what happened rather than as a comparison against what was intended.

And these decision records, which are not artifacts the user reads daily — they
exist precisely to hold the account that the artifacts must not carry, in the
one place where narrating is the job.
